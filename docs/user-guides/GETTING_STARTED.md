## Account Setup

### 1. Registration

1. Visit https://quantumtrade.io
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and create a strong password
4. Complete your profile information
5. Verify your email address

### 2. KYC Verification

For full trading access, complete KYC verification:

1. Go to Profile → Verification
2. Upload required documents:
   - Government-issued ID
   - Proof of address
   - Selfie for identity verification
3. Wait for approval (usually 24-48 hours)

### 3. Deposit Funds

**Fiat Deposits:**
1. Go to Wallet → Deposit
2. Select your currency (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.)
3. Choose payment method:
   - Bank transfer
   - Credit/debit card
   - Wire transfer
4. Follow instructions to complete deposit

**Crypto Deposits:**
1. Go to Wallet → Deposit
2. Select cryptocurrency
3. Copy your deposit address
4. Send funds from your external wallet
5. Wait for confirmations

## Making Your First Trade

### Step 1: Select a Market

1. Navigate to Markets tab
2. Search for your desired asset (e.g., "BTC")
3. Click on the trading pair (e.g., "BTC/USD")

### Step 2: Analyze the Market

Review the trading interface:
- **Price Chart**: Historical price movements
- **Order Book**: Current buy/sell orders
- **AI Insights**: Price predictions and sentiment
- **Recent Trades**: Latest executed trades

### Step 3: Place an Order

**Market Order** (executes immediately at current price):
1. Select "Market" order type
2. Choose "Buy" or "Sell"
3. Enter quantity
4. Review total cost
5. Click "Buy BTC" or "Sell BTC"

**Limit Order** (executes at specific price):
1. Select "Limit" order type
2. Choose "Buy" or "Sell"
3. Enter quantity
4. Set your desired price
5. Click "Buy BTC" or "Sell BTC"

### Step 4: Monitor Your Order

- Open orders appear in "Orders" tab
- Filled orders move to "Trade History"
- Positions appear in "Positions" tab

## Risk Management

### Setting Limits

1. Go to Settings → Risk Management
2. Configure:
   - Maximum position size
   - Maximum leverage
   - Daily loss limit
   - Stop-loss preferences

### Stop-Loss Orders

**Setting Stop-Loss:**
1. When placing order, select "Stop-Loss" type
2. Set stop price
3. Order triggers when price reaches stop

**Trailing Stop:**
1. Select "Trailing Stop"
2. Set trailing distance
3. Stop adjusts with favorable price movement

### Margin Calls

If margin ratio falls below threshold:
1. Receive notification
2. Add funds or close positions
3. Avoid liquidation
